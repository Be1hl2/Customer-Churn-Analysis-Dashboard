Customer Churn Analysis Dashboard
📌 Project Overview

Developed a multi-page Power BI Customer Churn Dashboard to analyze customer churn patterns, identify key churn drivers, and support executive decision-making through interactive analytics. The dashboard combines customer demographics, service usage, contract details, churn reasons, and revenue impact to uncover actionable insights for improving customer retention.

The objective of the project was to identify:

✔ Customer segments with the highest churn
✔ Service and contract factors influencing churn
✔ Revenue at risk due to customer attrition
✔ Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) impact
✔ City-wise churn performance
✔ Key business reasons driving customer churn
✔ Executive retention opportunities

The dashboard enables interactive and decision-oriented analysis through dynamic filters, KPI monitoring, and multi-page drill-down capabilities.

📊 Dashboard Features
Customer Churn Overview

The dashboard provides executive-level KPIs for monitoring customer retention performance:

Total Customers
Churned Customers
Retained Customers
Churn Rate
Average Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
Revenue at Risk

These KPIs provide a consolidated view of customer retention performance and business impact.

Service & Contract Analysis

Dedicated analysis pages were developed to evaluate:

Churn by Contract Type
Internet Service Analysis
Online Security & Tech Support
Payment Method Analysis
Customer Demographics
Month-to-Month Contract Risk

This analysis helps identify the operational and service-related factors contributing to customer churn.

Churn Reason Analysis

A dedicated dashboard was created to evaluate:

Top Churn Reasons
Revenue at Risk by Churn Reason
Churn Reasons by Internet Service
Churn Reasons by Contract Type

This analysis validates customer-reported churn reasons while supporting deeper business investigation.

City & Executive Risk Analysis

The dashboard provides strategic insights through:

City-wise Churn Performance
Revenue at Risk by City
Customer Risk Profiling
Executive Risk Dashboard
Retention Strategy Recommendations

This enables business leaders to prioritize high-risk customer groups and retention initiatives.

🔍 Key Business Insights

Some of the major findings from the dashboard include:

Month-to-Month contracts record the highest churn.
Fiber Optic customers show significantly higher churn.
Customers without Online Security or Tech Support are more likely to churn.
Senior Citizens exhibit higher churn than non-senior customers.
Competitor offers and customer support issues are the leading churn reasons.
Revenue at Risk highlights the financial impact of customer attrition.
🎯 Business Impact & Recommendations

The dashboard supports data-driven decision-making by helping stakeholders:

Identify high-risk customer segments
Prioritize retention strategies
Reduce customer churn
Protect Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV)
Monitor revenue at risk
Improve service offerings and customer experience

Recommended focus areas include:

Encouraging long-term contracts
Improving Fiber Optic service quality
Bundling Online Security and Tech Support
Strengthening customer support
Designing targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer groups
